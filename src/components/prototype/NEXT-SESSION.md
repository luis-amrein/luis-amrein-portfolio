# Magazine ideas for next time

Saved 21 October 2026. These are ideas to explore, not changes to implement yet. The order follows Luis's notes, rather than a priority ranking.

1. **Desk setting on the landing page.** Place the closed magazine against a desk background, with directional light and shadows as if illuminated by a desk lamp.
2. **Dotted cover pattern.** Explore a dotted pattern on the magazine cover.
3. **More magic through motion and video.** Explore animations and videos that make the magazine feel magical; one concrete example is a spinning star on the cover.
4. **Two levels of navigation.** Use the primary navigation to reach Work, then offer additional navigation through the individual projects within the Work section.
5. **Performance improvements.** Improve the prototype's loading and interaction performance, including page turns.
6. **A link from the original portfolio.** Add “Check out magazine” to the original portfolio when the magazine is ready to be linked.
7. **More shades of the original colours.** Explore a wider tonal range of the existing palette, including a potentially darker cover.
8. **Light and dark modes through a desk-light switch.** On the landing page, place a physical-looking light switch in one corner of the screen. Let visitors turn the desk lamp on and off to change between light and dark appearances. Explore having the lamp switch on when the page first loads.
9. **An interactive vinyl player in the opposite corner.** Show part of a vinyl record in the other corner of the screen, with a movable needle. Placing the needle on the record starts the music.

Keep the current closed-cover → portrait-left / contents-right opening flow as the starting point. Detailed motion fine-tuning remains deferred.
