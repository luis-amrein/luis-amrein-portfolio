### Final generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: background photograph for an interactive magazine lying on a real desk. Primary request: a completely empty, photorealistic wooden tabletop seen straight down from directly overhead, with warm light coming from above. Wide landscape composition, approximately 16:10, enough surface for an open magazine. Material: beautiful medium-dark natural walnut, restrained warm brown and honey undertones, real fine open pores, irregular flowing horizontal wood grain, extremely subtle wear and tiny scratches, satin finish with a broad soft highlight, natural variation rather than a repeated pattern. Lighting: a warm 3000K desk light outside the upper edge of the frame, above the scene, making a broad, softly feathered pool of light across the upper center and center, gently falling into deeper brown at the lower corners. Keep the central and right areas usable for a magazine overlay. Professional still-life photography, physically believable microtexture and subtle reflected light, tack-sharp wood detail, natural exposure, not orange-tinted. The entire image is ONLY the uninterrupted flat wood surface. No magazine, no paper, no objects, no lamp or switch in the frame, no text, no borders, no watermark, no desk edges, no room, no camera perspective, no illustration, no CGI look, no giant knots or decorative plank gaps.
