## The situation

Persephone is an internal platform for operations, sales, and key account management in the compliance carbon market. A client portal adds transaction tracking and creates opportunities for relevant follow-on sales.

I joined two months after launch as the first dedicated internal Product Manager. The product worked in parts, but the team needed a shared direction, a repeatable delivery rhythm, and a way to balance urgent operational needs with product improvements.

## How I approached it

My role spanned product direction and day-to-day team enablement. With two front-end and two back-end engineers plus the engineering lead, I established enough structure to make progress visible and decisions explicit.

- Acted as Product Owner and Scrum Master for the cross-functional team.
- Built and prioritised a roadmap tied to business goals and user needs.
- Turned operational pain points into PRDs, wireframes, and testable increments.
- Improved existing workflows while defining new client-facing capabilities.
- Conducted quality checks during delivery and clarified acceptance criteria.
- Managed communication with internal teams and clients, including onboarding and feedback.
- Supported business decisions with complex data queries and accessible analysis.

## Product judgment

Internal tools are often evaluated only by whether they technically work. I focused instead on the cost of friction: repeated manual work, unclear transaction status, and avoidable coordination between teams.

The roadmap balanced reliability work with improvements that made the platform more useful to clients. That helped prevent the client portal from becoming a disconnected surface and kept it grounded in the same operational truth as the internal tool.

## What changed

The team gained a prioritised roadmap, clearer product documentation, stronger quality checks, and one product contact for internal and client stakeholders. Delivery became easier to coordinate because product decisions and expected outcomes were made explicit.

## Tools

Jira, Confluence, Figma, Miro, and MongoDB Compass.

## What I would add now

This case study needs updated operational metrics, a concrete before-and-after workflow, and shareable examples of the client portal.
