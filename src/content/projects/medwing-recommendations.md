## The situation

MEDWING needed a better way to bring existing healthcare professionals back to relevant opportunities. Generic re-engagement created activity, but personalisation offered a stronger proposition: show each person jobs that fit their profile and intent.

I helped lead the project from inception with the Product Manager, owning data, analytics, and key delivery work.

## How I approached it

The service depended on both recommendation logic and the quality of job data. I treated measurement and enrichment as part of the product rather than supporting tasks.

- Defined KPI dashboards so performance was transparent across teams.
- Analysed engagement and conversion to validate the effect of personalisation.
- Led six working students who set up web crawlers and enriched job postings with dynamic content.
- Coordinated project phases and dependencies across contributors.
- Managed stakeholder expectations and shared regular performance updates.
- Connected campaign performance to broader acquisition economics.

## Product judgment

A recommendation is only as useful as the information behind it. Improving the matching concept without improving the job content would have produced precise links to thin or outdated opportunities.

That is why the enrichment operation mattered: it increased the amount and freshness of information available to the recommendation service and made the experience more useful after the click.

## What changed

Personalised re-engagement campaigns converted at three times the previous rate. By bringing existing users back through relevant, organically sourced opportunities, the service also reduced reliance on paid acquisition.

## Tools

Jira, Confluence, Looker, Metabase, Excel, and web crawlers.

## Reflection

The project showed how product operations and data quality can unlock a customer-facing result. The recommendation itself was visible; the system for gathering, enriching, and measuring the underlying data made it work.
